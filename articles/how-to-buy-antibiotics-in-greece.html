How to Buy Antibiotics in Greece

Visiting Greece and need antibiotics? Here’s what tourists should know about prescriptions, Greek pharmacies and getting medical help in Athens.

Do you need a prescription for antibiotics in Greece?

Yes. If you develop a sore throat, ear pain, urinary symptoms or another illness on holiday, the first step is a medical assessment. You cannot legally buy oral antibiotics over the counter in Greece.

Greek prescribing rules require a medical prescription for antibiotics. Electronic prescribing is the standard route, with specific exceptions provided by law. A doctor can explain which prescription arrangements apply to your circumstances.

This includes amoxicillin, amoxicillin/clavulanic acid (Augmentin), azithromycin, doxycycline, ciprofloxacin and cefuroxime. Knowing the name of a medicine or having used it before does not replace a valid prescription.

How can tourists get antibiotics in Greece?

1. See a doctor. The doctor will review your symptoms, examine you and decide whether an antibiotic is needed. Bring identification, a list of medicines and allergies, and any relevant previous prescriptions or results.

2. Arrange a home visit in Athens. If you are staying in a hotel, Airbnb or holiday rental, a doctor can assess you at your accommodation. A visit may include a medical history, examination, appropriate tests when available, treatment advice and a prescription when medically indicated.

3. Visit a hospital or medical centre. These are also options if you need assessment. For breathing difficulty, a severe allergic reaction, confusion or another life-threatening emergency, call 112 immediately.

Once you have an appropriate prescription, take it to a pharmacy. The pharmacist checks the prescription, availability and dispensing requirements. A consultation does not guarantee an antibiotic prescription.

Can I use a prescription from my own country?

Prescriptions from another EU country can generally be used in Greece if they contain the required information and meet local dispensing rules. A medicine may have a different brand name or may not be available.

Ask your prescriber for a cross-border prescription containing your full name and date of birth; the issue date; the prescriber’s name, qualification, work address, direct contact details and signature; and the medicine’s active ingredient, form, strength, quantity and dosage.

Do not assume your electronic prescription will work abroad. Check compatibility before travelling and ask for a paper cross-border prescription when needed.

If your prescription was issued in the UK, USA or another non-EU country, check with a Greek pharmacy or doctor before relying on it. Bring it to your consultation as a record of your treatment; a locally valid prescription may be needed.

Can I buy Augmentin or amoxicillin without a prescription?

No. Both are prescription antibiotics in Greece. The appropriate medicine depends on the diagnosis, your allergies, other medicines and individual risks.

Many sore throats and respiratory infections are viral, and some ear infections do not need antibiotics. Unnecessary antibiotics can cause adverse effects and promote resistance. Do not use leftovers or share antibiotics.

What if I have symptoms of a urinary tract infection?

Burning when urinating, frequent or urgent urination and lower abdominal discomfort can be symptoms of a urinary tract infection. A doctor can assess the likely cause and whether urine testing or a culture is needed.

Seek prompt medical attention for fever, chills, pain in your side or back, vomiting, or feeling significantly unwell. These may indicate a more serious infection.

Where can I find an open pharmacy in Athens?

Outside usual opening hours, designated pharmacies cover evening, overnight, weekend and holiday periods. In Greek they are called «Εφημερεύοντα φαρμακεία» — on-duty pharmacies.

Frequently asked questions

Can tourists buy antibiotics over the counter? No. Oral antibiotics require a valid medical prescription in Greece.

Can a pharmacist prescribe antibiotics instead of a doctor? A pharmacist cannot replace the required medical prescription for these antibiotics.

Can I get antibiotics for a sore throat? Only if a clinician determines that they are appropriate. A viral sore throat does not benefit from antibiotics.

Can I order antibiotics online for my Airbnb? Prescription medicines cannot be sold online to the public in Greece. A medical home visit is a consultation; it does not replace the required prescription and pharmacy dispensing process.

Can I use my European prescription? Generally, an EU cross-border prescription can be accepted if it meets the requirements. Electronic compatibility and medicine availability vary.
